Privacy Policy — Lolo's Farm

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Lolo's Farm is made by Cogsprite. The game runs on your device and works offline. It has no in-app purchases. It can show optional rewarded video ads (only when you tap a "watch an ad" button), and you can optionally sign in with Google Play Games to keep a copy of your farm in your Google account. This page explains what that means for your data.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (your farm, the farmer nickname and look you chose, your level and coins) and your settings are kept in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the game or starting a new farm deletes them. The farmer name is a nickname you type for the game; we never receive it.

Optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

Ads only appear when you choose to watch one for a small in-game reward, a few times a day at most. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Because players of all ages enjoy the game, every ad request is marked as child-directed: ads are non-personalised, limited to a general-audience rating, and the app does not use the Android advertising ID.

To deliver and measure an ad and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may process limited technical information such as your IP address, device and operating-system type, app version, and whether an ad was shown or tapped. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. If you are in the EEA or UK you can review your ad privacy choices from Settings → Privacy choices (ads) in the game.

Optional Google Play Games sign-in and cloud save

If you sign in with Google Play Games, the game receives your Play Games player ID and display name so it can show who is signed in. A copy of your game progress is saved in your own Google account using Play Games Saved Games, so you can restore your farm on a new phone. Cogsprite does not run a server and does not receive this data; it stays between your device and your Google account, under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in, and you can delete saved game data from the Google Play Games app settings.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example when your crops are ready). Nothing is sent to a server.

What we do not do

No analytics, no crash-reporting service, no in-app purchases, no selling or sharing of personal data, and no access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Deleting your data

Lolo's Farm (by Cogsprite) keeps no copy of your data on any server of ours, so you can remove everything yourself:

  • Game progress on your phone: uninstall the game, or open Settings → Apps → Lolo's Farm → Storage → Clear storage. This deletes your farm, nickname and settings immediately.
  • Cloud save (only if you signed in): open the Google Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data (or remove just Lolo's Farm's saved data there). This deletes the copy of your farm stored in your Google account and your Play Games player ID for the game.
  • Ad data held by Google AdMob: managed by Google under its own retention rules; you can manage it at myaccount.google.com.

If you'd like help, or want us to confirm a request, email support@cogsprite.com with the subject "Delete my data". We reply within 30 days. We keep no other data, so nothing is retained after these steps.

Children

Lolo's Farm is suitable for children. Ads are child-directed and non-personalised, there is nothing to buy, and signing in is optional. A parent or guardian who has a question or wants data removed can contact us at the address below.

Third-party services

Google AdMob (rewarded ads) and Google Play Games Services (sign-in and cloud save), both provided by Google LLC.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address and the "Last updated" date above will change.

سياسة الخصوصية — Lolo's Farm

لعبة Lolo's Farm من تطوير Cogsprite. اللعبة تعمل على جهازك وبدون إنترنت، ولا تحتوي على مشتريات داخلية. يمكنها عرض إعلانات فيديو بمكافأة اختيارية (فقط عندما تضغط زر مشاهدة إعلان)، ويمكنك اختياريًا تسجيل الدخول بـ Google Play Games لحفظ نسخة من مزرعتك في حسابك على Google.

ما الذي يُحفظ على جهازك

تقدّمك في اللعبة وإعداداتك تُحفظ في التخزين الخاص بالتطبيق على جهازك، وتُحذف عند إزالة اللعبة أو بدء مزرعة جديدة. اسم المزارع اسم مستعار ولا يصل إلينا.

الإعلانات الاختيارية (Google AdMob)

الإعلانات تظهر فقط عندما تختار مشاهدتها مقابل مكافأة صغيرة، وعدد مرات محدود يوميًا. كل طلب إعلان مُعلَّم بأنه موجّه للأطفال: إعلانات غير مخصّصة، بتصنيف مناسب للجميع، والتطبيق لا يستخدم معرّف الإعلانات. قد تعالج مكتبة AdMob معلومات تقنية محدودة (مثل عنوان IP ونوع الجهاز ونسخة التطبيق وما إذا ظهر الإعلان) لعرض الإعلان ومنع الاحتيال، وذلك وفق سياسة خصوصية Google.

تسجيل الدخول والحفظ السحابي (اختياري)

عند تسجيل الدخول بـ Google Play Games تحصل اللعبة على معرّف اللاعب واسم العرض، وتُحفظ نسخة من تقدّمك في حسابك على Google. نحن لا نملك خادمًا ولا نستلم هذه البيانات. يمكنك اللعب بدون تسجيل دخول.

حذف بياناتك

لا نحتفظ بأي نسخة من بياناتك على خوادمنا. لحذف تقدّمك: أزل اللعبة أو امسح بياناتها من إعدادات الهاتف. ولحذف الحفظ السحابي: من تطبيق Google Play Games ← الإعدادات ← حذف حساب وبيانات Play Games. ولأي مساعدة راسلنا على support@cogsprite.com بعنوان "Delete my data" ونرد خلال 30 يومًا.
